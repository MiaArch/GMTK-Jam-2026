Decisions:
- An event occurs and two/three decisions are given, each with different effects
- Cards are spawned with the given decisions
- Players can select a card and an effect will be applied


Architecture:
- DecisionCardManager: Controls cards and executes effects when a card is selected
- DecisionCard: Acts as storage of data such as title, text and effect. Players interact with this
- DecisionData: Data fed into the Decision Cards
- DecisionEvent: Holds the event scenario and the related decisions
- DecisionEffect: Used as basis to create effects such as GainCluesEffect
